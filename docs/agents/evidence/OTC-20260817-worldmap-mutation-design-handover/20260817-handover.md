
## What this enables

The project can now design, rather than guess, a controlled larger-extent experiment. A successful later implementation could potentially increase both rendered map area and client-resident tile/world-state area, but changing 18/14 alone is not proven sufficient because Viewport, RenderProvider, Picker, Camera and parser/network constraints must remain coherent.
